# Approved concept 12 — Lilac Savings Pal

Approved October 10, 2026 for both Budgie apps.

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: brand-new Budgie budgeting app icon exploration aimed at young adults. Take a decisively youthful contemporary direction: bold playful digital-product identity, unexpected electric colors, very few big shapes, custom graphic character with attitude. Cute without being babyish. Youthful social app energy. Create ONE polished square opaque full-bleed app icon concept, one memorable centered symbol, 20% safe padding. Flat clean vector-style geometric forms, high contrast, no text or watermark, no phone mockup, no border or rounded exterior corners. Completely original artwork. Avoid heritage crests, ornamental feathers, detailed nesting twigs, beige, muted gold, forest green, stock-finance charts, dollar signs, gradients, texture, 3D, shadows. The bird and money-saving motif should feel like a single witty design, not clip art combined. Direction: LILAC SAVINGS PAL. Bright lilac #C3ADFF full background. One original front-facing soft-square vivid cobalt #3457FF parakeet mascot, shaped like a chunky rounded bean with a tiny off-center two-notch tuft at top. Two tiny near-black #171C35 vertical oval eyes and a short hot-coral #FF796B triangle beak. Its lower half is a big pale-lime #E8FF8B semicircular nest pocket with the upper edge curved like a broad smile, containing one coral circle coin partially peeking above it. Wings are two simple cobalt pill-shaped nubs that hug the sides of the pocket. Friendly goofy understated expression, oversized simple proportions, squishy geometric modern charm, emphatically youthful. No cheeks, feather markings, detailed nest lines, feet, gold or beige. A minimal flat logo not an illustration scene.
