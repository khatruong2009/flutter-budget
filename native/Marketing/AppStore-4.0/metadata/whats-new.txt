Meet Budgie 4.0 — a fresh start for the app, with your financial history coming along.

• A rebuilt native iPhone experience with redesigned navigation, cards, and charts.
• Two fresh looks: warm Paper and dark Midnight themes.
• Refreshed views for your dashboard, spending, savings goals, net worth, and cash flow.
• Home Screen widgets updated to match the new design.
• Improved VoiceOver support and layouts that adapt to larger text.
• More flexible categorization rules, including editing existing rules and setting amount ranges.
• Improved saving reliability and clearer feedback when a change needs to be retried.

Your existing transactions, budgets, goals, and net worth history carry over automatically. Open Budgie and pick up where you left off.
